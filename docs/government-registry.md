# Government registry concepts

These concepts distinguish an institution, person or physical asset from its registration, authorization and the record describing it. They are reference meanings, not a government submission format, full-domain model, normative specification or a conclusion about anyone's rights. The terms are authored across the organization, ownership, regulation, education, work, transport, environment, tax, elections and physical-asset modules and are at draft maturity. Where a French or Spanish translation is missing, the site falls back to English.

## Scope and evidence

The following dispositions account for the government domain families. The source standards informed the definitions; PublicSchema chooses the relationships, optional fields and serialization. None of these sources is claimed as an exact mapping or a complete implemented interchange.

Dataset wrappers are exchange packaging rather than independently meaningful domain concepts. They are intentionally not public classes. Likewise, national code inventories, workflow status enumerations and authority-specific required fields are profile content rather than a universal closed vocabulary.

## Modeling decisions and alternatives

All new fields are optional at reference-vocabulary level. Registrations inherit `registered_subject` (a URI), `registration_authority`, `registration_jurisdiction`, `valid_from` and `valid_to` from Registration. Professional licenses and driving entitlements further inherit Authorization. The URI permits Person or Organization subjects where the domain permits them without widening Party or accepting software agents as legal actors. A profile must verify target existence and permitted types; JSON Schema cannot establish that a URI resolves to a Person. The example profile below imposes person-only subjects for voter, driving and professional authorizations.

Codes use `CodedValue` and retain source code, scheme, optional source label and meaning URI. This was chosen over new national enum lists and over reusing Identifier for classifications. Unknown or retired local codes remain representable without assigning a fabricated universal meaning. The enum `InterestDirectness` describes the relation, not a jurisdiction's beneficial-ownership thresholds. The enum `FormOfWork` is the closed list of the five forms of work in the 19th ICLS resolution (paragraph 7); status at work stays a `CodedValue` because ICSaW-18 and national variants carry their own editions.

PublicSchema's existing `address` property explicitly refers to a household, `located_at` to a service point, `managed_by` to a service point operator, and `jurisdiction` to data protection law. Reusing these merely because the field names looked suitable would change their meaning. These modules therefore record building and facility addresses through dated asset address assignments, facility operators through asset party roles, and public competence through a competence area property. Existing name, identifiers, Person, Organization, dates and ServicePoint meanings are reused unchanged.

An environmental facility's operator is an `AssetPartyRole` whose subject is the facility, whose actor is a person, organization or group URI and whose role type codes operation. A facility operated by an individual does not require an invented Organization. Because the role is dated, a change of operator adds a role rather than changing the facility, which keeps its identity. [Regulation (EU) 2024/1244](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1244/oj/eng), which applies from 1 January 2028, recognizes natural-person and legal-person operators in Article 3(2); its definition groups installations on a site by operator, so this identity rule is a deliberate difference.

A second option was to create Person subclasses named Taxpayer, Voter and Practitioner. That obscures canonical identity and the jurisdictional, dated nature of recognition. Another option was copying every registry's specialized containers and records. That conflates exchange envelopes, legal records and real-world subjects. PublicSchema instead specializes relationships when meaning warrants it and uses shared registration, authorization, evidence and record constructs.

Not every statement about a government registry becomes a new universal class. Business filings, professional test evidence and registration certificates can be described through the shared evidence/record layer. Asset roles have optional reference concepts because their identities and meanings exist independently of workflow. Detailed pharmaceutical manufacturing stages and recursive packaging encodings remain with the corresponding interchange profile; PublicSchema does not flatten these into its simpler quantities or free text. These reference concepts answer selected questions; no lossless projection back to a source registry is claimed.

The generic AwardedQualification, ProfessionalLicense and PracticeRole concepts do not replace native Practitioner or PractitionerRole for medical exchange. FHIR Practitioner.qualification includes licenses as well as academic qualifications, so these are not exact field mappings. PracticeRole is a close match for PractitionerRole; AwardedQualification is a close match for the ELM LearningAchievement, not for Practitioner.qualification.

For medical meaning, use the selected native FHIR R5 5.0.0 resource and profile.
PublicSchema has no native medicinal product, healthcare service or accreditation classes.
Keep medical detail in FHIR and link it to PublicSchema registry and shared identities where
that is independently useful. [Native FHIR records in a PublicSchema registry](fhir-registry-integration.md)
documents the synthetic example, its official-shape checks and the known mapping losses.

Tax and other representation uses RepresentationRole, whose representative is a person or organization and whose represented party can also be a group or legal arrangement; it does not assume that representation happens inside an organization. RegistryEntry can describe an OwnershipInterest assertion with its recording time, source records and evidence assertions. The interest remains the dated subject of that statement, and recording it never verifies its truth. Several statements may concern the same interest without merging source identity.

## Examples and checks

`examples/government-domains/records.json` contains synthetic records spanning the government classes. Synthetic entity identifiers use `https://example.org/`. A company and a public agency remain organizations; separate interests identify natural and institutional holders. Education uses a virtual site without an address. A person holds two category entitlements with different expiry dates. The same vehicle is referenced by a registration and inspection. A site's compliance assessment and regulatory action remain separate, and its operator and address are dated AssetPartyRole and AssetAddressAssignment records. The [ownership and education guide](government-relationships.md) covers synthetic ownership routes and offerings.

Run `uv run --locked pytest tests/test_government_domains.py` from the repository root. The tests build the real catalog and production RDF/JSON-LD/SHACL exporters, validate the same fixtures in JSON Schema and context-expanded SHACL, and assert every authored class and slot survives. Negative fixtures test primitive/cardinality/code rejection in both public representations. The separately named **example profile**, `uv run --locked python examples/government-domains/profile.py`, rejects unresolved or wrong-kind subjects, empty or reversed end-exclusive periods, compact or impossible dates, repeated record identities and incoherent percentage bounds. These profile checks are local rules, not enforced by the reference vocabulary (see [the examples README](../examples/README.md)).

The ownership percentage slots are bounded to zero to one hundred in the vocabulary. The vocabulary expresses no cross-field conditions, such as an exact value excluding bounds or a lower bound not exceeding an upper bound; the example profile checks interval coherence. Legal eligibility, code membership, unit conversions and applicable regulatory thresholds are likewise profile validation.
