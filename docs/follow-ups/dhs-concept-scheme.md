# DHS concept scheme — what we did, what we didn't

`schema/concept_schemes/dhs_household.yaml` is auto-generated by
`build/seed_metrics_from_dhs_api.py`. It mints one `publicschema:concept/dhs/{slug}`
per distinct DHS Level2 bucket present in the kept indicator set, by lowercasing
the Level2 label and replacing non-alphanumeric runs with underscores. Every
`Metric` in `schema/metric_catalog/dhs_household.yaml` carries a `concept_uri`
pointing at the corresponding minted URI.

This exercises the intent of the `concept_uri` slot
(`schema/metrics.yaml` lines 533-545): multiple metrics may share one concept URI
when they measure the same underlying notion through different
operationalisations. The 12 "Number of usual members" indicators (mean,
percent with N members, etc.) now all share
`publicschema:concept/dhs/number_of_usual_members`.

## What this is NOT

A curated SKOS concept scheme.

- Concept granularity is fixed at DHS Level2. The Level2 taxonomy was authored
  by ICF for DHS report tabulation, not for cross-survey ontology work.
- No SKOS `broader` / `narrower` / `exactMatch` relations.
- No human review of whether a Level2 label is concept-coherent.
- Slugs are mechanical: `agricultural_ownership` is what you get, not what an
  ontologist would pick.
- No cross-linking to UN P&R 2020, OECD glossary, or Core Person.

## Buckets flagged `likely_mixed`

The converter flags 12 buckets where Level2 demonstrably bundles multiple
concepts. These should be split before being treated as authoritative:

| Bucket | Metric count | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Age of population | 29 | Spans every age band 0-4 through 95+; each band is plausibly its own concept. |
| Educational attainment of the household population | 39 | Attainment levels (none, primary, secondary, higher) crossed with sex. |
| Background characteristics | 6 | Mixes residence, region, age, wealth — these are dimension values masquerading as a concept. |
| Household sanitation facilities | 44 | Facility type, sharing status, and JMP improved-status are distinct concepts under one bucket. |
| Children's living arrangements and orphanhood | 15 | Living arrangement type and orphanhood status are independent concepts. |
| Children 10-14 living arrangements and orphanhood | 17 | Same as above for an older age band. |
| Agricultural ownership | 2 | Land ownership and farm-animal ownership are independent concepts. |
| Clean fuels and technologies | 13 | Clean-fuel use for cooking, space heating, and lighting are bundled into one Level2; each is a distinct domain. |
| House ownership and documentation | 28 | Ownership status and title-deed status are independent concepts; the Level2 label itself joins both with "and". |
| Household effects | 8 | Asset-index components (radio, TV, telephone, computer, refrigerator) under one umbrella label. |
| Lighting fuel or technology | 42 | The Level2 label itself bundles fuel and technology with "or". |
| Older age persons | 9 | Mixes HH-composition wrt elders ("HH with members 65+", "HH with only members 65+", "HH with 3 generations"), non-nuclear family composition ("Population living in non-nuclear families"), and de jure 65+ population. |

The remaining 19 buckets were reviewed by reading their metric labels and
appear single-concept (with operationalization variation via HH-level vs
population-level views, fuel/technology types, and distribution buckets).
Two borderline cases were not flagged but warrant note:

- **Household characteristics** (6 metrics): contains only denominators
  (HH total, population total, weighted/unweighted counts). It is not a
  substantive concept; a curation pass should likely move these out of
  the concept scheme into per-metric denominator metadata.
- **Household means of transport** (5 metrics): structurally identical to
  `Household effects` (one row per transport asset: bicycle, motorcycle,
  car, cart, motorboat). Kept as single-concept because DHS scopes it as
  "transport-asset ownership"; a stricter curation could split by asset
  type for parity with `Household effects`.

## Why we didn't curate now

A real curation pass requires demographic / SP-statistics expertise and a
decision about cross-survey alignment (DHS vs MICS vs UN P&R). The seed's
purpose is to exercise the `Metric` + `concept_uri` shape end-to-end with
publicly defended provenance. Curating would have:

1. Slowed the seed work.
2. Locked in concept choices before the schema sees real query patterns.

## Curation pass scope (when undertaken)

1. Read each `likely_mixed: true` bucket and propose splits, with metric-to-new-concept
   reassignment. Rerun the converter with the override table.
2. Confirm or rename the 19 unflagged concepts. Many slugs (e.g.
   `frequency_of_smoking_in_the_home`) are acceptable. Resolve the two borderline
   cases above (`household_characteristics`, `household_means_of_transport`).
3. Add SKOS relations across the scheme and into PublicSchema's existing
   vocabularies (`schema/vocabularies.yaml`).
4. Cross-link to authoritative external schemes where they exist: UN P&R 2020
   Census recommendations, OECD population glossary, MICS indicator manual.
5. Extend the same auto-mint + curation pattern to the ILO SDG seed
   (`schema/metric_catalog/ilo_sdg.yaml`), which currently has no `concept_uri`
   values at all.

## Stability contract

Slugs are stable across runs as long as the DHS API does not rename a Level2
bucket. The mechanical rule means: any rename in the DHS API invalidates URIs.
A future curation pass should snapshot the mapping into a manual override table
so URIs survive upstream label changes.
