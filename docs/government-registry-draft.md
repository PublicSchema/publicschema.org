# Government registry concepts: draft for contribution

These concepts distinguish an institution, person or physical asset from its registration, authorization and the record describing it. They are proposed reference meanings, not a government submission format, full-domain model, normative specification or a conclusion about anyone's rights. The described additions are draft, including terms authored across the government, assets and qualified-relationship modules. The English text is the current review text; missing French and Spanish use the site's normal fallback and prevent maturity promotion until reviewed translations exist.

## Scope and evidence

The following dispositions account for the government domain families. The source standards informed the definitions; PublicSchema chooses the relationships, optional fields and serialization. None of these sources is claimed as an exact mapping or a complete implemented interchange. The sources were consulted on 7 September 2026.

Address, health facility, agricultural holding, cadastral land and shared registry foundations are addressed in the companion draft, not duplicated here. Dataset wrappers are exchange packaging rather than independently meaningful domain concepts. They are intentionally not public classes. Likewise, national code inventories, workflow status enumerations and authority-specific required fields are profile content rather than a universal closed vocabulary.

## Modeling decisions and alternatives

All new fields are optional at reference-vocabulary level. Registrations inherit a subject URI, authority, jurisdiction and validity dates from Registration. Professional licenses and driving entitlements further inherit Authorization. The URI permits Person or Organization subjects where the domain permits them without widening Party or accepting software agents as legal actors. A consuming profile must verify target existence and permitted types; JSON Schema cannot establish that a URI resolves to a Person. The example profile below imposes person-only subjects for voter, driving and professional authorizations.

Codes use `CodedValue` and retain source code, scheme, optional source label and meaning URI. This was chosen over new national enum lists and over reusing Identifier for classifications. Unknown or retired local codes remain representable without assigning a fabricated universal meaning. The sole new enum, `InterestDirectness`, describes the relation, not a jurisdiction's beneficial-ownership thresholds.

PublicSchema's existing `address` property explicitly refers to a household, `located_at` to a service point, `managed_by` to a service point operator, and `jurisdiction` to data protection law. Reusing these merely because the field names looked suitable would change their meaning. This module therefore uses explicit building/facility addresses, environmental operator, and governmental area properties. Existing name, identifiers, Person, Organization, dates and ServicePoint meanings are reused unchanged.

A second option was to create Person subclasses named Taxpayer, Voter and Practitioner. That obscures canonical identity and the jurisdictional, dated nature of recognition. Another option was copying every registry's specialized containers and records. That conflates exchange envelopes, legal records and real-world subjects. The draft instead specializes relationships when meaning warrants it and uses shared registration, authorization, evidence and record constructs.

Not every statement about a government registry becomes a new universal class. Business filings, professional test evidence and registration certificates can be described through the shared evidence/record layer. Building parts, asset roles and road restrictions have optional reference concepts because their identities and meanings exist independently of workflow. Detailed pharmaceutical manufacturing stages, recursive packaging encodings and executable road-routing rules remain with the corresponding interchange profile; the draft does not flatten these into its simpler quantities or free text. These reference concepts answer selected questions; no lossless projection back to a source registry is claimed.

For medical meaning, use the selected native FHIR R5 5.0.0 resource and profile. The
native medicinal, service and accreditation draft classes are retired. Preserve medical
detail in FHIR and link it to PublicSchema registry and shared identities where that is
independently useful. Root `Substance` also serves environmental data and requires a
separate reuse decision. [Native FHIR records in a PublicSchema registry](fhir-registry-integration.md)
documents the current 19-resource synthetic example, its official-shape checks, and the
remaining mapping questions.

## Examples and checks

`examples/government-domains/records.json` contains synthetic records spanning the government classes. Synthetic entity identifiers use `https://example.org/`. A company and a public agency remain organizations; separate interests identify natural and institutional holders. Education uses a virtual site without an address. A person holds two category entitlements with different expiry dates. The same vehicle is referenced by a registration and inspection. A site's release, compliance assessment and regulatory action remain separate. The companion [qualified relationships guide](government-relationships-draft.md) covers synthetic ownership routes, offerings and installation attribution.

Run `uv run pytest tests/test_government_domains.py` from the repository root. The tests build the real catalog and production RDF/JSON-LD/SHACL exporters, validate the same fixtures in JSON Schema and context-expanded SHACL, and assert every authored class and slot survives. Negative fixtures test primitive/cardinality/code rejection in both public representations. The separately named **example profile** tests unresolved or wrong-kind subjects, incoherent periods and exact percentage bounds. These profile checks are test support, not enforced by the reference vocabulary or a RegistryStack runtime.

Percentage bounds and paired strength rules are deliberately profile validation: the current catalog JSON Schema writer does not export LinkML numeric bounds or cross-field conditions. The vocabulary does not advertise enforcement that only one exporter implements. The same applies to legal eligibility, road topology, code membership, unit conversions and applicable regulatory thresholds.

## Contribution brief

The intended reader is a registry implementer deciding which identity and assertion a field describes. Contributions should provide a contradictory example, terminology correction, authoritative source, or concrete alternative relationship, with a synthetic fixture. Highest-value review questions are:

- Can a non-person, non-organization legal arrangement hold an ownership interest in your jurisdiction? The present endpoint policy supports persons and organizations and does not pretend to resolve trusts or arrangements without legal personality.
- Which provider establishment structures are independently identified apart from provider and site? GIAS distinguishes groups and establishments, but its national categories are not universal. The virtual-site generalization is a PublicSchema design choice, supported by the need to avoid inventing premises, not a claimed GIAS field equivalence.
- Are intervals or minimum/maximum bounds essential for your ownership exchange? The exact scalar and qualified description preserve that distinction but do not compute on bounds.
- Which regulatory inspection outcomes require assertion source, evidence and finality beyond the shared record layer? Supply a profile rule rather than assuming a finding is final.
- Which FHIR resource/profile and cross-sector consumer need govern a medical link? Supply the source release/profile, consuming query and any information that remains unmapped. The current example demonstrates bounded R5 shape validation, not a clinical or jurisdictional conformance claim.
- Which building parts and traffic restrictions need shared identities beyond the bounded asset/network model? Provide the geometry encoding, temporal scope and downstream question before expanding the vocabulary.

External domain review and adopter interchange demonstrations remain needed for maturity promotion. No outside reviewer was contacted and no live registry compatibility is asserted by these local examples.

## Incorporated review

Maintainer review accepted optional reference identities for structural BuildingPart, dated vehicle actor roles and road restrictions where their meanings are independently needed. Medical product classes were subsequently retired in favour of native FHIR R5 records. RepresentationRole also corrects the initial overly narrow assumption that tax representation always occurs inside an organization. RegistryEntry can describe an OwnershipInterest assertion with recording time, source records and evidence assertions; the interest remains the dated subject of that statement, and recording it never verifies its truth. Several statements may concern the same interest without merging source identity.
