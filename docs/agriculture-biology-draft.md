# Agricultural biology draft review

This bounded draft distinguishes biological identity, managed populations, animal movement, cultivation episodes and physical plant material. All twelve concepts and their new properties are draft. It is neither a full agricultural-biology model nor a normative specification. Terms specific to agriculture use `agri/`; shared biological identities such as `IndividualAnimal`, `AnimalGroup`, `PlantVariety`, `GeneticResourceAccession`, `SeedLot`, `AnimalResponsibility` and `AnimalMovement` retain root URIs by meaning. The seven source families are implemented; no registry dataset containers or application-required fields are imported. See [synthetic identity and relationship records](../examples/agriculture-biology/records.json), [movement records](../examples/agriculture-biology/movement-records.json) and `tests/test_agriculture_biology.py`.

| Family | Decision and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Individual animal | `IndividualAnimal` persists when a tag is replaced. Reuse `Identifier`, not the human `Person` or its sex vocabulary. Birth year preserves uncertain precision. `AnimalResponsibility` separates dated keeper and owner roles; `AnimalResidence` separately dates the place where the animal is kept. |
| Animal group | `AnimalGroup` is independent of the human `Group` hierarchy. `AnimalGroupCount` dates population observations. `AnimalMovement` separately records an actual movement, its known individual participants and number moved. Its source group identifies the managed population from which animals were drawn, without asserting that every member moved. Predecessors can describe splits or merges but do not establish their event chronology. |
| Animal breed | `AnimalBreed` identifies a source-contextual breed concept. A national breed population and its risk assessment are observations, not timeless breed properties. |
| Crop planting | `CropPlanting` is cultivation, not an administrative declaration or permanent land attribute. `CropPlantingComponent` supports mixed crops with distinct dates. Area components may overlap. Season, parcel, dates and area remain optional. |
| Plant variety | `PlantVariety` distinguishes biological identity from national listing, protection and denomination approval. Multiple names do not imply multiple varieties. |
| Genetic resource accession | `GeneticResourceAccession` covers plant collection accessions. Collection-scoped numbers and holding institute remain distinct. Neither a variety nor a physical sample is identical to an accession. Animal and microbial accession specialization is outside this plant family. |
| Seed lot | `SeedLot` identifies physical material. Multiple lots may represent one accession; mixtures can refer to multiple varieties, accessions and source lots. Quantity does not claim a current inventory balance. |

## Evidence and alternatives

[WOAH Terrestrial Code 2024, chapter 4.3, article 4.3.3, programme design 5, registration 3(c), "Other events"](https://www.woah.org/fileadmin/Home/eng/Health_standards/tahc/2024/en_chapitre_ident_design.htm) distinguishes animal movement from changes of owner or keeper that need not change establishment. It identifies movement dates, origin, destination, transit establishments and the number moved as traceability information. Individual and group identification and replacement identifiers are supported by the neighbouring identification and animal-registration provisions. A native movement event is therefore justified alongside residence and responsibility associations; it is not left solely to an application profile.

`AnimalMovement` specializes the existing `Event`. Its source-group reference and incomplete known-participant list are PublicSchema choices that distinguish a managed population from a quantity moved. We use departure as the occurrence date, without inventing an arrival date; `recorded_at` keeps entry time separate. Transit sites form an unordered collection, not an itinerary. All fields remain optional reference vocabulary. The narrower meaning is actual movement between physical sites. `Farm` identifies an economic production unit that may use multiple premises; its identity alone does not identify a departure or arrival site. Animals can move between premises within one holding without changing that holding's identity. No facility-to-holding relationship is inferred. WOAH notes national conventions that also classify births, deaths or ownership changes as movements; those conventions are not asserted by this class. Inferring travel from changed residence intervals would lose evidence about the actual movement, its participants and transit sites.

[FAO DAD-IS data](https://www.fao.org/dad-is/data/en/) distinguishes breed information, population size and structure, and risk information. We retain a contextual breed concept instead of treating a breed name as a globally unique key or importing current population status into its identity.

[FAO WCA 2010, chapter 11, crop concepts](https://www.fao.org/4/a0135e/a0135e05.htm) discusses successive, interplanted and mixed crops and area attribution. This is historical conceptual evidence, not current census conformance. Components permit mixed episodes without mandating that crop areas sum to parcel area. A single primary-crop field would lose this information.

[UPOV 1991 Convention Article 1(vi)](https://www.upov.int/documents/d/upov/docs-en-publications-upov_pub_221.pdf) supplies the variety distinction. The actual [PLUTO TAG data format, pages 5–6, tags 010, 540–543 and 600](https://www.upov.int/documents/d/upov/docs-en-pluto-contribute-pluto-tag-format-description.pdf) separates national-list and rights records and supports differing denominations across countries. PublicSchema retains identity and names; approval history belongs to a jurisdictional registration profile. No equivalence to an entire PLUTO record is claimed.

The [Genesys API manual, sections 3.1–3.4](https://www.genesys-pgr.org/documentation/apis) provides an actual accession JSON model with institute, accession number, taxonomy and incomplete collection dates. Section 3.2 explicitly labels its JSON model outdated. It is used as historical implementation evidence, not a current API contract. Preserving the original date string avoids turning `1990----` into a fabricated January date. We do not copy its identity triplet as a universal uniqueness constraint.

[CFIA QSP 152.1, definitions and sections 4.6–4.9](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/plant-health/seeds/seed-inspection-procedures/oecd-and-eu) distinguishes lots, blends, mixtures and certification, including differing domestic and OECD terminology. Generic source-lot links are preferable to importing a jurisdiction-specific mixture enum. Composition proportions and certification rules require an explicit scheme profile; source links alone cannot reconstruct a regulated mixture declaration.

The supplied agriculture starter was compared with these sources. Its global registry inheritance, closed animal vocabularies, required season year and exact-looking field alignments were not carried over. Native global slots, typed relationships and open `CodedValue` classifications support review without locking one registry's operating rules into the vocabulary.

## Examples, counterexamples and export limits

The animal example changes keeper while retaining one animal identity, one continuing residence and the same owner. Separate dated relationship records preserve this distinction; changing a keeper never implies a sale or movement. `AnimalResidence` uses the `agri/` namespace and the migrated `start_date`/`end_date` convention, while the shared responsibility and movement concepts retain root identities. The synthetic herd has twelve animals but only one known individual. Summing both as thirteen double-counts livestock. The mixed planting has two independently dated components and no fabricated season. Two seed lots refer to one accession and variety; merging these three identities destroys physical traceability. A partial variety with no protection is valid vocabulary data.

The separate movement example records three animals drawn from a herd previously counted at twelve. Two herd members are individually known, but only one is identified as a movement participant; the other remains at the origin. The event does not fabricate identities for the other two moved animals, move every known member, or calculate a remaining population of nine. A market and a transit centre are identified without a residence assertion or claimed visit order. The moving animal retains its identity and owner, with separate residence assertions before and after travel. Removing the movement record leaves those residence assertions valid but supplies no movement evidence.

JSON Schema exports check primitive types, arrays and date formats when a format checker is enabled, but accept additional properties and unresolved string references. SHACL additionally checks typed RDF relationships, including moved individuals and the source group, when target nodes and their types are supplied. URI-only residence subjects, sites and actors, and movement sites, require a consuming profile to resolve and check their stated target types. Neither export proves identity, pedigree, varietal purity, completeness or chronological consistency. The vocabulary does not encode submission-requiredness or numeric bounds. A single transit URI represented as a JSON scalar fails the array schema even though RDF has no scalar-versus-array distinction.

The focused count-submission test requires a nonnegative population observation and date. The runnable `examples/agriculture-biology/validate_movement_profile.py` separately requires a movement identity, departure date, positive headcount, origin, destination, and either known participants or a source group. It accepts partial individual identification, checks that identified participants do not exceed the headcount, and resolves references using only the supplied records. This demonstration accepts `AgriculturalFacility` premises as sites; other physical site types need an explicit profile extension. A `Farm` record does not satisfy that physical-site requirement. Missing or wrong-type targets fail this profile even when a native URI slot passes both exports. It checks date and timestamp syntax, without comparing a calendar date to a timestamp or inferring a timezone. Run it with `uv run --locked python examples/agriculture-biology/validate_movement_profile.py`. No whole-starter or RegistryStack compatibility claim is made.

No external standard enum or exact mapping is asserted. Original local or unmapped codes can be carried in `CodedValue` without fabricating an external meaning URI. Translation and independent terminology review remain prerequisites for maturity promotion.

## Contribution brief

Please provide contradictory examples or source-backed alternatives for breed recognition across communities, perennial planting episode boundaries, uncertain parentage, accession identity after transfer, and mixed-lot composition. For movement, review the departure-date convention, source-group interpretation, journeys combining multiple source groups, and cases needing timed or ordered transit visits. The bounded event does not describe those richer itineraries, transport allocation or automatic inventory reconciliation. Reporting procedures and movement-management systems remain operational concerns. Denomination approval history, pedigree confidence, seed tests and access-and-benefit-sharing status remain separately identified specialization questions, not claims delivered by these identity concepts. External review has not yet occurred.
